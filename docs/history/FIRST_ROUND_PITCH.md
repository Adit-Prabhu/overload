# First-round pitch draft
Historical first-round draft. Submission status unverified.

**Title:** Overload

**The X:** My game is a top-down shooter where you steal and fuse enemy powers, and you feel clever when a new combination works.

**Player and motivation:** I am designing for players who enjoy fast combat and experimenting with abilities. The motivation is discovery, and you'd see it when players explore hidden rooms and try different loadouts.

**Core loop:** You fight enemies and collect powers. The game offers upgrades and fusion options. You test combinations, see what works, and choose what to equip before the next fight.

**The agent:** A recurring rival learns your combat habits and changes its tactics, limited to what it observes and a predefined set of moves. I compare it with a rival following fixed attack patterns.

**What the player loses:** With the scripted rival, you lose the satisfaction of changing your approach to surprise an opponent that has learned your habits.

The later scope draft narrowed the concept to three base powers, two fusion combinations and a small number of observed combat habits. The class-feedback attribution was hypothetical, not verified.
