## What this covers
- The rendered audio in `assets/audio/` (music stems, stings and sound effects).
- The images in `docs/art/` (reference renders, contact sheets and screenshots).
- The art the game draws: sprites, tiles, effects and HUD, as rendered on screen.

## What it does not cover
- **Source code**, including the code in `src/overload/art/` and `tools/` that draws the art and
  composes the audio. Code is under the MIT license in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **The Silkscreen font** in `assets/fonts/silkscreen/`, by Jason Kottke. It keeps its own
  SIL Open Font License 1.1, in `assets/fonts/silkscreen/OFL.txt`.
- **Dependencies** such as pygame-ce (LGPL 2.1), which keep their own licenses.

`assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` lists every file under `assets/` with its license.
